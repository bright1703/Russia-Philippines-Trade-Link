FOR PHILIPPINE IMPORTERS & BUSINESS PARTNERS

Planning to sign a contract with a Russian supplier?

Before starting cooperation, Philippine companies can request an independent background review of a Russian supplier or business partner.

We help collect and analyse publicly available information about the company, including its registration status, business history, legal disputes, bankruptcy indicators, enforcement proceedings and other available signs of counterparty reliability.

The review can be requested not only for suppliers presented through Russia–Philippines Trade Link, but also for a Russian company independently selected by the Philippine buyer.

What the review may cover

  • Company registration and current legal status
  • Founders, directors and ownership information where publicly available
  • Company operating history and basic business profile
  • Arbitration and court cases
  • Bankruptcy and enforcement information
  • Signs of liquidation, reorganisation or suspended activity
  • Public financial and business-risk indicators
  • Available export experience and product information
  • Comparison of the supplier’s declared information with available public records
What you receiveThe client receives a structured background summary with identified risk factors, open questions and practical points to clarify before signing a contract or making an advance payment.

This is an independent information review based on available public and legally accessible sources. It is not a government certificate, legal opinion, audit or guarantee of future performance. Findings are framed as risk indicators, information gaps and points requiring additional verification.

How the review works and which sources are used

Request a Counterparty Check

Send your company name, contact person and preferred way to reach you. We will clarify the remaining details in a follow-up.

We review publicly available and legally accessible information only. We do not promise access to closed databases, bank information or confidential law-enforcement data.
Possible public sources may include
  • Russian state registration and tax records
  • Public court and arbitration records
  • Bankruptcy and enforcement databases
  • Public corporate and business databases, including SPARK or SBIS where access is available
  • Company websites, official documents and open business information
  • Other legally accessible public sources

Reports avoid categorical conclusions such as “fraudulent company”. They identify available signals, open questions and matters that may require enhanced due diligence or independent legal and financial verification.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked
questions.

Short answers about how supplier matching, documents and background reviews work.

Do you sell the products yourself?

No. Russia–Philippines Trade Link is run by an independent trade agent based in the Philippines. The role is to find a suitable manufacturer, collect current information and connect the buyer and the supplier. Commercial terms, the supply contract and payment are agreed directly between the buyer and the supplier.

Can every Russian product be imported into the Philippines?

Not automatically. Requirements depend on the product: food, meat products, fertilizers and equipment are regulated differently. Document and permit questions are reviewed at the start of each inquiry, and final import eligibility should be confirmed by the importer with the relevant Philippine authorities or a licensed customs broker.

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the product and the manufacturer. Minimum lots, packaging, pricing basis and delivery terms are requested from the supplier once the buyer's product, volume and delivery point are known.

Can you check a Russian company that I found myself?

Yes. The background review is available for any Russian company, not only for suppliers presented on this website. It is based on public and legally accessible sources and is not a legal opinion or a guarantee.

What information should I send with an inquiry?

The product or category, required volume or frequency, packaging preferences, the delivery point in the Philippines and any certificates or documents your business needs. The more specific the request, the faster a suitable supplier can be identified.
