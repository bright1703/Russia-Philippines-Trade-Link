Issue of 28 September 2026

What this means. The news is mixed: pork imports are being tightened, while demand for poultry and rice is rising, and there are separate signals on rehabilitation equipment and agri-drones. The key point for Russian suppliers is pork, where quotas and higher tariffs are under discussion, so check the current tariff regime, permits, and frozen meat inspection rules before shipping. For poultry, demand is steady but competition and local output are growing, so prepare documents and pricing with logistics in mind, and watch customs enforcement changes and BAI ASF consultations because they affect delays and market access.

Issue of 24 September 2026

What this means. The main signal is Philippine demand for imported meat: pork cuts and offal are driving purchases and chicken imports are expected to keep growing, so Russian suppliers should focus on importers and processors in those categories. The backdrop is getting harder, since the EU deal gives European suppliers preferential access and the zero-tariff regime for some goods is ending, which will raise price competition. Local hog groups want import policy reviewed, so rules can shift and decisions by the Bureau of Animal Industry are worth watching. Also expect stricter customs enforcement on misdeclared shipments and softer retail demand as growth slows.

Issue of 21 September 2026

What this means. The items here are a mixed bag, but for Russian sellers the key one is Miratorg clearing the Philippine inspection audit and already shipping meat, which shows the demand is real and the approval path is workable. On pork, check with your buyer which tariff code and rate will apply, since the reclassification of frozen pork jowls is still undecided and duties depend on it. Poultry and pork imports are forecast to grow, while rice is already locked up by Vietnam and under a safeguard investigation, so it is a poor fit. Watch the Philippines-Canada free trade talks, because a deal would give Canadian suppliers preferential terms and affect your pricing.
