STATUS · FOR PHILIPPINE BUYERS AND PARTNERS

Our status and documents.

Russia–Philippines Trade Link is run by Andrey Subich, an independent trade agent based in the Philippines, working under the "Trade Agent of Primorsky Krai" project. Not a registered company.

Information updated 23 October 2026.

Who we work with

On 10 July 2026 a cooperation agreement was signed between the Ministry of International and Foreign Economic Relations of Primorsky Krai, the Primorsky Krai Entrepreneurship Support Center and Andrey Subich. Its subject is helping Primorsky Krai businesses enter the Philippine market. The Center has informed the Embassy and the Trade Representation of the Russian Federation in the Philippines.

This is an independent, non-government website working within a Primorsky Krai export support program. It is not an official website of the Government of Primorsky Krai or the Russian Federation.

What we do and don't do

  • We introduce Philippine buyers to selected Primorsky Krai manufacturers and help with first contact and documents.
  • We don't sell goods, hold your money or sign contracts for either side. Terms and payment are agreed directly between you and the supplier.

How we are paid

Our work as a trade agent is supported under a program of the Primorsky Krai Entrepreneurship Support Center, so buyers pay nothing for supplier matching. Separately, we offer paid consulting, such as a public-records review of a Russian company. If a supplier signs a separate commission agreement with us, we tell the buyer.

AGREEMENT
on cooperation between the Ministry of International and Foreign Economic Relations of Primorsky Krai, the autonomous non-profit organisation "Primorsky Krai Entrepreneurship Support Center" and Andrey Vladimirovich Subich, citizen of the Russian Federation

Vladivostok, 10 July 2026

The Ministry of International and Foreign Economic Relations of Primorsky Krai, represented by the Minister, Konstantin Gennadyevich Sidorenko, acting on the basis of the Regulations on the Ministry of International and Foreign Economic Relations of Primorsky Krai approved by Resolution of the Government of Primorsky Krai No. 949-pp of 27 December 2024;

the autonomous non-profit organisation "Primorsky Krai Entrepreneurship Support Center", represented by its General Director, Ivan Aleksandrovich Mashunin, acting on the basis of the Charter of the autonomous non-profit organisation "Primorsky Krai Entrepreneurship Support Center";

and Andrey Vladimirovich Subich, citizen of the Russian Federation, acting as an individual under the special tax regime "Professional Income Tax",

hereinafter referred to as the "Parties", have concluded this agreement (the "Agreement") as follows.

Article 1

The subject of the Agreement is cooperation between the Parties to assist entrepreneurs of Primorsky Krai in entering the market of the Republic of the Philippines.

Article 2

Within their competence and in accordance with applicable law, the Parties intend to cooperate on the following matters related to the subject of this Agreement:

  • raising the awareness of Primorsky Krai entrepreneurs of opportunities to enter the market of the Republic of the Philippines;
  • providing analytical, consulting, reference and information support on matters within the Parties' sphere of activity;
  • providing information, consulting and other support to Primorsky Krai entrepreneurs;
  • research of the market and the competitive environment of the Republic of the Philippines;
  • assistance in preparing and concluding export contracts.

Article 3

The Parties will cooperate in the following main areas:

  • information, methodological and consulting interaction aimed at involving Primorsky Krai entrepreneurs in entering the market of the Republic of the Philippines;
  • organising joint events, including conferences, seminars and other public events focused on the Philippines;
  • developing agreed proposals on creating favourable conditions for Primorsky Krai entrepreneurs doing business in the market of the Republic of the Philippines;
  • monitoring the conditions for doing business in the market of the Republic of the Philippines;
  • increasing the activity of Primorsky Krai entrepreneurs in developing trade with the Republic of the Philippines and building a positive public view of promoting products on a foreign market;
  • exchanging information on business matters and on the current and planned activities of the Parties.

Article 4

The Agreement enters into force when signed by the Parties.

The Agreement may be supplemented and amended by mutual consent of the Parties by signing an additional agreement. All supplements and amendments form an integral part of the Agreement, are made in writing and must be signed by duly authorised representatives of the Parties.

The Agreement may be terminated by agreement of the Parties by signing an additional agreement. It may also be terminated unilaterally at the initiative of one of the Parties by written notice to the other Parties sent no later than 30 (thirty) calendar days before the termination date. In that case the Parties' obligations under contracts and agreements concluded in connection with the implementation of this Agreement remain in force for the term and on the conditions set by those contracts and agreements.

Article 5

In all matters not provided for by the Agreement, the Parties are guided by applicable law.

The Parties undertake not to disclose confidential information that became known to them in the course of cooperation, except as provided by applicable law.

The Agreement does not create any financial or other property obligations for the Parties. The Agreement is not a preliminary contract, an agreement on the conduct of negotiations or a joint activity contract. The Parties do not undertake to conclude other contracts (agreements) on its basis in the future and may not compel each other to do so in court.

The Parties acknowledge that neither at the time of signing nor in the future does this Agreement restrict them from signing contracts (agreements) with subjects and purposes similar to this Agreement, either between the Parties or with any organisations operating in the Russian Federation or abroad, public law entities or other legal persons.

The Agreement will be implemented in accordance with the principles of open competition. It is not aimed at restricting the Parties' cooperation with other persons and does not seek to restrict the activities of other organisations or persons.

The Agreement does not affect the rights and obligations of the Parties under other agreements (contracts) to which they are party.

The Parties intend to resolve all disputes and disagreements arising from the implementation of the Agreement through negotiations.

The Agreement is made in 3 (three) copies of equal legal force, one original for each Party.

Dear colleagues,

We inform you that on 10 July 2026 a cooperation agreement was signed between the Ministry of International and Foreign Economic Relations of Primorsky Krai, the autonomous non-profit organisation "Primorsky Krai Entrepreneurship Support Center" and Andrey Vladimirovich Subich, citizen of the Russian Federation. Under this agreement, assistance to Primorsky Krai entrepreneurs in entering the market of the Republic of the Philippines has been organised.

Andrey Vladimirovich Subich leads this direction under the "Trade Agent of Primorsky Krai" project and provides practical support to Primorsky Krai companies that are looking for partners in the Republic of the Philippines, assessing market opportunities and preparing to enter the local market.

His tasks include:

  • analysing the prospects for supplying products of Primorsky Krai enterprises to the Philippine market;
  • searching for importers, distributors and business partners;
  • preliminary assessment of Philippine companies' interest in cooperation;
  • organising business contacts and negotiations;
  • exchanging information on regulation, industry specifics and business practice in the market of the Republic of the Philippines.

We would be grateful for working interaction with Andrey Vladimirovich Subich on promoting the products and services of Primorsky Krai companies on the Philippine market, and on other matters of developing trade and economic cooperation.
